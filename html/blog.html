Why your first project should be boring

Ambitious first projects stall. A small, complete build teaches more about structure, state, and shipping than a half-finished idea ever will.

We see this every cohort: the learner who wants to build a social network in week two and the learner who quietly finishes a to-do list with real persistence. Six weeks later, the second learner is the one debugging a data model with confidence — because they’ve already shipped something end to end.

  • Small scope forces you to finish
  • Finishing teaches more than starting
  • Boring projects leave room to learn deeply
